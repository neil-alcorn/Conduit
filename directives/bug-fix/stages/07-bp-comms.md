# Stage 7 — BP Comms (Bug Fix)

## ⛔ COMMIT DISCIPLINE

**Do NOT commit or push.** The Conduit window commits after gate approval.

**Stage 7 is skipped for bug-fix convoys.**

Bug fixes do not require a business communications stage unless the bug had significant external-facing impact and a separate communication decision was made at Stage 0. In that case, use the net-new directive as a guide.

See `directives/net-new/stages/07-bp-comms.md` for reference on what this stage covers in net-new convoys.
