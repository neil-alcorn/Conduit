# Stage 7 — BP Comms (Maintenance)

## ⛔ COMMIT DISCIPLINE

**Do NOT commit or push.** The Conduit window commits after gate approval.

**Stage 7 is skipped for maintenance convoys.**

Maintenance convoys do not require business communications unless a dependency update or infra change produces a user-visible behavior change. In that case, reclassify the convoy as enhancement before proceeding.

See `directives/net-new/stages/07-bp-comms.md` for reference on what this stage covers in net-new convoys.
